Nene primer

(backspace closes this · D scrolls down · U scrolls up)

Nene sends letters through your four fingers — V (index), B (middle), N (ring), M (little). Every letter is either a tap (quick) or a touch (held), on one or two fingers at once.

Letters

  • A–J — tap 1 or 2 fingers together
  • K–T — touch (hold) 1 or 2 fingers together
  • U–Z — hold one finger down, tap a second finger while it's held

Space, backspace, period

  • Space — touch V, tap B
  • Backspace — touch V, tap N
  • Period — touch N, tap M

Modes

  • Digit mode — touch V, tap M
  • Alpha mode (back to letters) — touch B, tap M

Alt — everything else lives here

Touch B, tap N to open Alt. Tap Alt again right away for Enter. Or type a word first (T, W, H, Q, C, X, N, CLEAR so far), then close with Alt again to run it:

  • T — highlights the "Share this" button below; tap it to send what you typed (text, email, anywhere)
  • P — highlights the "Speak this" button below; tap it to hear what you typed read back
  • W — open a web tab
  • H — this primer
  • Q — question mark
  • C — comma
  • X — exclamation point
  • CLEAR — wipe everything typed so far
  • N — open NT1 (phrase practice)

Backspace works mid-word if you fumble a letter — nothing sends until you close with the second Alt.
