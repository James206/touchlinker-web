What It Is

A new channel
for human language.

Touchlinker™ develops haptic communication technology enabling silent, eyes-free language transmission through vibrotactile finger patterns. Our patented single-gesture encoding system delivers full language through four finger actuators simultaneously — no screen, no sound, just touch.

The encoding system — Nene™ — maps 47 characters to natural finger patterns learnable in four minutes. Tap, touch, and compound gestures across four fingers create a complete haptic alphabet. Bidirectional. Wireless. Always available.
